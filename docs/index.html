Privacy Policy

Loopkeep · Last updated: July 30, 2026

Loopkeep (“the app”) is a home maintenance tracking application. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Data Collection

Loopkeep does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. All information you enter into the app — including item names, dates, notes, and reminders — is stored locally on your device only.

No Internet Connection Required

The app functions entirely offline. It does not send any data to external servers, and the developer has no access to any information you enter.

Notifications

Loopkeep uses your device's local notification system to remind you of upcoming maintenance tasks. These notifications are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device and are not sent through any external service.

Third-Party Services

Loopkeep does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

Loopkeep does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13, as no information is collected at all.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in the future (for example, if account creation or cloud sync is added), this page will be updated and the app's store listing will reflect the change.
